# Company news for the 2024–2025 final test

The position monitor (part of the adopted book, Decision 110d) acts on company headlines
that arrive during a trade. FNSPID, the development source, ends in 2023.

## Decision (Decisions 116–116d)

| Candidate | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Alpha Vantage `NEWS_SENTIMENT` | **Premium only**: the free key cannot read historical news (two format probes, no headline read) |
| GDELT DOC 2.0 API | **Infeasible**: rate-limited (HTTP 429) after two queries, through every back-off |
| **TickerTick** | **Adopted, with a reported limitation** |

**Calibration** on the same 20 companies, 2023-01-01 to 06-30, with FNSPID's company-specific
title test, availability rule and FinBERT tone:

| | FNSPID | TickerTick |
|---|---|---|
| Company-specific headlines | 943 | 11,593 |
| Toned sessions (feed the monitor triage) | 288 | 1,392 (× 4.8) |
| Ticker-weeks with company news | 48 % | 92 % |
| Headlines with a time of day | 0 % | 99.9 % |

TickerTick is much denser. With it, the frozen monitor triage passes roughly five times more
checks per trade than in development, so the frozen exit rule gets more chances to fire. This
is reported with the final-test result; no threshold changes.

## Capture after the unlock

1. January 2024 to 25 May 2025: TickerTick's bulk file of 2025-05-26 (`tickertick_extract`).
2. 26 May to 31 December 2025: TickerTick's live API, paging back per ticker
   (`tickertick_api`, 10 requests a minute; about 5,000 requests, under 9 hours).
3. Normalise with `tickertick_frame` and write `data/external/news_final/headlines.parquet`;
   the desk reads it for the unlocked years only.

All functions are in `structural_break.intelligence.news_sources` and refuse dates after 2023
while the final test is locked. The 2025 bulk file is not downloaded before the unlock.

# Company news for the 2024–2025 final test (Decision 113)

FNSPID, the headline source used to build and tune the monitor and the
v3 desk, ends in December 2023. The final test needs company headlines
for 2024–2025, because the position monitor (part of the adopted book,
Decision 110d) acts on news that arrives during a trade.

**Rule:** no 2024–2025 news, prices or filings may be captured before the
owner's unlock record (Decision 42 gate). An adapter can be built and
checked on pre-2024 data only.

## Options (checked 2026-10-26)

| Source | 2024–2025 coverage | Access | Fit with FNSPID | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha Vantage `NEWS_SENTIMENT`** | 2022 onward, by ticker and time window | free key, 25 requests a day | medium | About 250 requests (one per ticker), so about 10 days. The 2022–2023 overlap with FNSPID allows a calibration check |
| **Multi-source HF corpus** (`Brianferrell787/financial-news-multisource`) | S&P 500 headlines through 2024, plus 2025 Yahoo Finance and NYT feeds | HF login and access request, non-commercial | good for 2024, thinner for 2025 | Tickers sit in `extra_fields` for some sources only. Day-level stamps |
| FINSABER-data (Apache-2.0) | 2024 only, headlines keyed by trading date | download (11 GB) | medium | No 2025 |
| Finnhub free tier | last 12 months only (from about Oct 2025) | free key | poor for 2024 | Good for a live or forward paper book |
| SEC 8-K filings | yes | existing pipeline | exact | Already the evidence for the 8-K news exit |

## Recommendation

1. **8-Ks:** capture 2024–2025 8-K filings through the existing SEC pipeline,
   unchanged.
2. **Headlines:** a 2024–2025 source is needed in addition. Preferred:
   - **Alpha Vantage** with a free key, which gives one source for 2024 and
     2025;
   - or the **multi-source HF corpus**, if the access request is granted.
3. **Calibration, before unlocking, on 2022–2023 only:**
   - pull the same tickers from the new source for 2022–2023;
   - compare company-specific headline counts and the FinBERT triage pass
     rate with FNSPID.

   A large difference changes how often the monitor is called, so it is
   reported as a limitation, and the monitor threshold stays frozen.

**Needed from the owner:** an Alpha Vantage key (free) or HF access to the
corpus.
